Operating Philosophy

How I think about systems

My work is rooted in a belief that the best systems are the ones people actually use — designed with the user in mind, built for scale, and measured by adoption not features.

Read more about me →
01

Simplicity scales

Complex systems that nobody adopts solve nothing. The best operational systems are simple enough to use without training.

02

Adoption over features

A system with 3 features that 100% of the team uses beats a system with 30 features that 10% of the team understands.

03

AI should reduce friction

AI augments human decision-making by eliminating the low-value tasks that drain cognitive bandwidth and slow execution.

04

Systems should fit humans

People adapt to bad systems. Good systems adapt to people — meeting workflows where they actually exist, not where they should be.
